How We Build Your Day

Ceremony, cocktail hour, reception.

For the ceremony itself, we keep it simple and elegant — a soft instrumental prelude while guests are seated, a processional that actually sounds like a processional, and a recessional with a little more lift to send everyone into cocktail hour. One or two saxophones is usually plenty here; you don't need six horns for a twenty-minute ceremony, and we'll tell you that upfront instead of upselling you into more than the moment calls for.

Cocktail hour is where the saxophone-group format really shines — jazz standards, easy funk, a little bossa nova, music that fills the room without competing with conversation. Once dinner service starts we dial things back down, then bring the full group back in as the night moves toward dancing, building toward a set that actually gets people on the floor.

Good to Know

Wedding FAQ.

How many musicians do we need for our wedding?

It depends on your ceremony and venue size. One or two saxophones is usually plenty for a ceremony, while the full six-piece group brings more energy for cocktail hour and reception. We're happy to help you figure out what fits.

Can you learn our first dance song?

Yes. If there's a song that matters to you, tell us in advance and we'll learn it.

How far do you travel for weddings?

We're based in Tampa and regularly play across central Florida, including St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota, Lakeland, and Orlando. We'll travel further for the right date.

How do we book you for our wedding date?

Fill out our booking form with your date, venue, and event details, and we'll get back to you within a day or two with availability and next steps.
