How We Build Your Day

Ceremony, cocktail hour, reception.

For the ceremony, we keep things simple — a soft prelude while your guests take their seats, a processional that actually sounds like one, and a recessional with just enough lift to carry everyone into cocktail hour. It's still all six of us up there, we just play it quiet and unhurried until it's time to open things up.

Cocktail hour is where things really start to move — jazz standards, a little funk, some bossa nova, music that fills the room without talking over anyone's conversation. We ease off again once dinner's served, then bring the energy back up as the night turns toward dancing, building to a set that actually gets people out of their chairs.

Good to Know

Wedding FAQ.

Does the whole band play during the ceremony, or just part of it?

All six of us are there the whole time — we just play the ceremony quieter and simpler than the reception. Same band, different volume.

Can you learn our first dance song?

Yes — if there's a song that matters to you, let us know ahead of time and we'll have it ready.

How far do you travel for weddings?

We're based in Tampa and play all over central Florida — St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota, Lakeland, Orlando, you name it. Further out, just ask.

How do we book you for our wedding date?

Fill out our booking form with your date, venue, and a few details about the day, and we'll follow up within a day or two with availability and next steps.
