How We Build Your Day

Ceremony, cocktail hour, reception.

For the ceremony, we keep it simple and beautiful. Soft prelude while guests arrive, a processional that actually sounds like one, a recessional with enough life to carry everyone forward. All quiet and intentional.

Cocktail hour is where we open up. Jazz standards, some funk, bossa nova — music that moves the room but still lets people talk. We ease back once dinner starts, then bring things up again as the night shifts toward dancing. By the end of the night, we're playing sets that actually get people on the dance floor.

Good to Know

Wedding FAQ.

Does the whole band play during the ceremony?

Yes. All of us are there the whole time. We just play it simpler and quieter for the ceremony, then open up from there.

Can you learn our first dance song?

Absolutely. Let us know ahead of time and we'll have it ready.

How far do you travel for weddings?

We're based in Tampa and cover central Florida — St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota, Lakeland, Orlando. If you're further out, just ask.

How do we book you?

Fill out our booking form with your date, venue, and some basic details about your day. We'll follow up within a day or two with availability and pricing.
