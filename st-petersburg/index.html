St. Petersburg FAQ.

Do you actually travel to St. Petersburg, or are you only based in Tampa?

We're based in Tampa but play in St. Pete regularly — it's a quick trip across the bay and one of the markets we cover most often, alongside Clearwater and Sarasota.

Is there an extra travel fee for St. Petersburg events?

St. Petersburg is within our standard central Florida coverage area, so there's typically no extra travel fee. Send us your venue and we'll confirm as part of your quote.

What kinds of St. Petersburg events do you play?

Weddings, corporate events, festivals, and private parties — indoors or outdoors, waterfront or downtown. We're an acoustic horn section, so venues with sound restrictions are usually easier for us than for a full amplified band.

How do we book you for a St. Petersburg date?

Fill out our booking form with your date, venue, and event type. We'll follow up within a day or two with availability and pricing.
