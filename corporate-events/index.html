How We Fit In

Music that knows when to hang back.

Live horns pull attention in a way a playlist doesn't. People notice, they lean in, they drift over. We're smart about it though. During the business parts of your event, we're playing something easy that doesn't fight for space. Once it's time to celebrate, that's when we bring the energy. We read the room and adjust accordingly.

We show up early, we dress for the occasion, and we're easy to work with. No backing tracks, no complicated setup. Just solid musicians who know how to handle a professional event.

Good to Know

Corporate Event FAQ.

Can you match the tone of a formal corporate event?

Totally. We do a lot of background sets for professional receptions and networking events, and we can bring the energy up once it's time to celebrate.

Do you provide sound equipment?

For smaller rooms we bring our own amplification. For bigger venues, we can plug into house sound if you have it available. Just let us know what you're working with.

How much notice do you need?

Sooner is better, especially for December when holiday parties book up fast. But reach out even on short notice — you might get lucky.

How do we get a quote?

Fill out our booking form with your event details and we'll follow up within a day or two.
