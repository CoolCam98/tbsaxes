How We Fit the Room

Music that reads the room.

Live horns cut through a crowded activation or launch event in a way a playlist just can't — they draw people in, hold attention at a booth or step-and-repeat, and give your brand moment an actual pulse. We adjust volume and energy to fit foot traffic and the flow of the space.

We show up on time, dress appropriately for the room, and can scale between a background-level presence and a full performance depending on what the event calls for. No pre-recorded tracks, no laptop on stage — just a group of musicians who know how to read a corporate room.

Good to Know

Corporate Event FAQ.

Can you match the tone of a formal corporate event?

Yes — we regularly play polished, background-level sets for receptions and networking events, and can scale up the energy for celebrations later in the evening.

Do you provide your own sound equipment?

For smaller indoor settings we can bring our own amplification. For larger venues we're happy to work off house sound — just let us know what's available.

How much advance notice do you need for a corporate event?

The sooner the better, especially for December holiday parties, which book up early. That said, reach out even on short notice — availability might surprise you.

How do we get a quote?

Fill out our booking form with your event details and we'll follow up with real numbers within a day or two.

Let's book your event.
