How We Fit In

Music that knows when to hang back.

A playlist blends into the background at a launch or activation. Live horns don't — people notice, they wander over, they stick around your booth or step-and-repeat a little longer than they meant to. We keep an eye on the crowd and adjust volume and energy to match how the room's actually moving.

We show up on time, dress for the room, and know how to sit back during the quiet stretches and step forward once it's time to bring the energy up. No backing tracks, no laptop up front — six musicians who've done enough of these to know the difference between a keynote reception and a holiday party.

Good to Know

Corporate Event FAQ.

Can you match the tone of a formal corporate event?

Yes — we play a lot of polished, background-friendly sets for receptions and networking events, and we can bring the energy up once it's time to celebrate.

Do you provide your own sound equipment?

Depends on the space. For smaller rooms we bring our own amplification, and for bigger venues we're happy to plug into house sound — just tell us what's available.

How much advance notice do you need for a corporate event?

Sooner is always better, especially for December — holiday parties book up fast. That said, reach out even on short notice; you might get lucky.

How do we get a quote?

Fill out our booking form with the event details and we'll follow up with real numbers within a day or two.
