Corporate

Live Saxophone for Corporate & Brand Events.

Corporate events need music that can do two jobs at once — sound sophisticated enough for a room full of clients or executives, and still bring real energy when the event calls for it. A saxophone group hits both without needing a stage, a full PA rig, or an hour-long soundcheck eating into your event timeline.

Brand Activations & Product Launches

Live horns cut through a crowded activation or launch event in a way a playlist just can't — they draw people in, hold attention at a booth or step-and-repeat, and give your brand moment an actual pulse. We adjust volume and energy to fit foot traffic and the flow of the space.

Holiday Parties & Company Celebrations

Year-end parties and company celebrations are where we get to stretch out. Background jazz during a cocktail reception, easing into funk and soul as the night goes on, then a full dance set once the room's ready for it. We read the crowd instead of running a fixed script.

Conferences & Networking Receptions

During registration, breaks between sessions, or a closing networking reception, we keep things polished and unobtrusive — present enough to elevate the room, quiet enough that conversation and business still happen easily.

What to Expect

We show up on time, dress appropriately for the room, and can scale between a background-level presence and a full performance depending on what the event calls for. No pre-recorded tracks, no laptop on stage — just a group of musicians who know how to read a corporate room.

Booking a Corporate Event

Every quote is built around group size, set length, and location, so we don't post a flat rate here — reach out with your event details and we'll get you real numbers within a day or two. December fills up fast with holiday parties, so if you're planning one, the earlier you reach out the better.
