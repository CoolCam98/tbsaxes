How We Work

One band, every kind of event.

We're five saxophones and a drummer, and we scale to fit the room. A duo for a quiet ceremony, the full six-piece for a dance floor, or anything in between — we figure out what your event actually needs instead of showing up with a fixed package.

No backing tracks, no laptop on stage. Every note you hear is played live, which means we can shift genre and energy in real time — dial it down for a networking reception, bring it up for a wedding reception, or hold a festival crowd's attention for a full set. The four pages below break down how that looks for specific kinds of events, but the short version is: tell us what you're planning, and we'll tell you how we'd approach it.

Good to Know

Services FAQ.

How do we know which service fits our event?

Start with what kind of event you're planning — a wedding, a corporate party, a festival slot, or a surprise visit — and reach out with the details. We'll help you figure out group size and set length from there.

How far do you travel?

We're based in Tampa and regularly play across central Florida, including St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota, Lakeland, Brandon, and Orlando. We'll travel further for the right date.

How do we get pricing?

Every quote is built around group size, set length, and location, so we don't post flat rates. Fill out our booking form with your event details and we'll follow up with real numbers within a day or two.

Can you combine services, like a Sax-O-Gram at a corporate holiday party?

Absolutely. Tell us what you're picturing and we'll help you build it — plenty of our bookings mix and match.
