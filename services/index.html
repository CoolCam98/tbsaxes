How We Work

One band, every kind of event.

No click tracks, no pre-recorded anything, no laptop running the show. We're actually listening to what's happening around us and adjusting in real time. If you need us quieter during dinner and louder once dancing starts, we're already on it. That's the whole point of live music.

Good to Know

Services FAQ.

How do we know which service fits our event?

Start with what kind of event you're planning — a wedding, a corporate party, a festival slot, or a surprise visit — and reach out with the details. We'll help you figure out what fits from there.

How far do you travel?

We're based in Tampa and regularly play across central Florida, including St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota, Lakeland, Brandon, and Orlando. We'll travel further for the right date.

How do we get pricing?

Pricing depends on which service you're booking, how long you need us, and where — so we don't post flat rates here. Fill out our booking form with your event details and we'll follow up with real numbers within a day or two.

Can you combine services, like a Sax-O-Gram at a corporate holiday party?

Absolutely. Tell us what you're picturing and we'll help you build it — plenty of our bookings mix and match.
