Where Your Support Goes

Straight back into the community.

A good chunk of what we do doesn't pay us anything. Through We Support Local, we play for free at ribbon cuttings and grand openings for new local businesses — no cost to them, which means it comes out of our own pocket to make it happen.

Every so often we also show up somewhere just to give back — like the afternoon we spent teaching a group of kids the basics of playing saxophone. Donations don't fund a single show or a single band member — they help us say yes to more of these moments, not fewer.

Good to Know

Donation FAQ.

Where does my donation go?

Directly back into the community side of what we do — free performances through We Support Local, music education moments like teaching kids saxophone basics, and other one-off community appearances.

How much should I give?

Whatever feels right. Every bit helps us say yes to more of these.

Is this the same as booking you for an event?

No — donations support our free community work. If you want to hire us for a paid event, head to our booking page instead.
