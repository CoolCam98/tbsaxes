What to Expect

What a show feels like.

You're getting live musicians playing together in a room. We're not running a fixed setlist. We're listening to the crowd, to each other, and pulling songs and energy from wherever makes sense in the moment. Our public shows are where we test new material, play with friends' bands, or just show up somewhere good for the music. If you want to hear what we actually sound like before you book us, this is the way.
