What to Expect

What a show feels like.

Five saxophones and a drummer, playing live in a room together — no backing tracks, no pre-recorded anything. We move through jazz, funk, soul, Motown, and pop, reading the crowd and adjusting the set as we go instead of running a fixed script.

Our public shows are usually where we're testing new material, playing with friends' bands, or just showing up somewhere fun — they're a good way to hear us before booking us for your own event. Follow along below so you don't miss the next one.
