Why Live Works Outdoors

Real horns, real projection.

Whether you need a tight twenty-minute festival slot or a multi-hour patio residency, we build the set to fit your time and read the crowd as we go. Jazz, funk, soul, pop, R&B — we've got enough range to keep people listening whether we're opening the day or closing it out.

We also do a lot of recurring patio gigs at breweries and restaurants, so we know how to start the afternoon easy and build energy as the night goes on. We've got deep material, which means a three-hour set never feels repetitive.

Good to Know

Festival FAQ.

What set lengths can you play?

Anywhere from a tight twenty-minute slot to multi-hour residencies. We adjust the set to fit your time.

Do you need us to provide a stage or power?

Tell us what your venue looks like and what power access you have. We bring our own amplification for smaller stages and we're flexible with bigger setups.

Can you play multiple sets over a few hours?

Yes, we do multi-set bookings at breweries and patios all the time. We adjust energy and song selection as the day goes on.

How do we book a slot?

Fill out our booking form with your event details and we'll follow up with availability and pricing.

Let's book your stage.
