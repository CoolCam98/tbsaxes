Why Live Works Outdoors

Real horns, real projection.

Twenty minutes or two hours, we build the set to fit your slot and keep the energy moving the whole way through. Jazz, funk, soul, pop, R&B — that range is what keeps a festival crowd locked in whether we're opening the day or closing it out.

We also do a lot of recurring gigs on patios and outdoor seating areas — easy background music early, building toward something with more energy as the night goes on. We've got enough material that a multi-hour set never feels like we're repeating ourselves.

Good to Know

Festival FAQ.

What set lengths can you play?

Anywhere from a short 20-minute festival slot to a multi-hour residency set — we build the set list to fit your time.

Do you need us to provide a stage or power?

Depends on the venue. Tell us your stage size and power access and we'll work with what's available — we bring our own amplification for smaller stages.

Can you play multiple sets over a few hours?

Yes, we regularly play multi-set bookings at breweries and restaurant residencies, adjusting energy as the day goes on.

How do we book a festival slot?

Fill out our booking form with your event details and we'll follow up with availability and pricing.
