Festivals

Live Saxophone for Festivals & Outdoor Stages.

A six-piece horn section is built for outdoor energy. Real saxophones project across a crowd in a way backing tracks and small PA speakers never quite manage, and we adjust our set length and intensity to fit anything from a twenty-minute festival slot to a multi-hour residency.

Festival Stages

Short set, long set — we build the set list to fit your slot and keep the energy moving the whole time. Our genre range (jazz, funk, soul, pop, R&B) means we can hold a festival crowd's attention whether we're opening the day or closing it out.

Breweries & Restaurant Residencies

Recurring gigs on a patio or in an outdoor seating area are a great fit for this format — background-friendly during earlier hours, building toward a more energetic set as the night goes on. We're comfortable holding a room for a few hours without repeating ourselves.

Street Festivals & Community Events

Outdoor markets, art walks, block parties — anywhere people are moving through a space rather than seated in front of a stage, live horns are a great way to draw people in and hold a crowd for a few songs at a time.

What We Need From the Venue

Every outdoor setup is a little different. Tell us your stage size and power access and we'll make it work — for smaller setups we can bring our own amplification, and for larger festival stages we're happy to work off house sound.

Booking a Festival Slot

Every quote is built around group size, set length, and location, so we don't post a flat rate here — reach out with your event details and we'll get you real numbers within a day or two.
