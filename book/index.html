Good to Know

Booking FAQ.

How far in advance should we book?

The earlier the better, especially for Friday/Saturday dates between May and October, and for December holiday parties. That said, reach out even on short notice — availability might surprise you.

Is a deposit required to hold a date?

We'll go over deposit details once we've talked through your event and sent a quote — reach out and we'll walk you through it.

Can we request specific songs?

Yes. Tell us what matters to you — a first dance, a walk-in song, a favorite artist — and we'll work it into the set.

What areas do you travel to?

We're based in Tampa and regularly play across central Florida, including St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota, Lakeland, Brandon, and Orlando. We'll travel further for the right date.
