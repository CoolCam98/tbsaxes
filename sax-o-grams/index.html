How It Works

You tell us who, where, and why.

We show up — with as few as one or two players, or the full six-piece kit if you want to make a bigger splash — play a short set, and make somebody's day. No rehearsal needed on your end, just point us in the right direction and we'll handle the rest.

If you're coordinating a surprise, it's usually easiest for someone other than the guest of honor — a coworker, a partner, a friend — to handle the booking with us, so the moment stays a genuine surprise.

Good to Know

Sax-O-Gram FAQ.

How much notice do you need to book a Sax-O-Gram?

The sooner the better, but reach out even if your event is coming up soon — availability might work out.

Can someone else book it as a surprise?

Yes, that's how it usually works. A coworker, partner, or friend typically handles the booking so the guest of honor has no idea it's coming.

How many musicians come for a Sax-O-Gram?

As few as one or two, or the full six-piece group — whatever fits the moment and space you have in mind.

What areas do you cover for Sax-O-Grams?

We're based in Tampa and cover the greater Tampa Bay area and central Florida.
