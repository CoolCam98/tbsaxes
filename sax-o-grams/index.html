Surprise

Sax-O-Grams — A Surprise Worth Remembering.

Think of it as a singing telegram, but with saxophones. Book us to show up somewhere — an office, a backyard, a restaurant table — and play a short surprise set for someone who has no idea it's coming.

How It Works

You tell us who, where, and why. We show up — with as few as one or two players, or the full six-piece kit if you want to make a bigger splash — play a short set, and make somebody's day. No rehearsal needed on your end, just point us in the right direction and we'll handle the rest.

Great For

  • Birthdays
  • Work anniversaries & retirements
  • Holiday office surprises
  • Proposals
  • Just because

A Few of Us, or the Whole Kit

Sax-O-Grams are built to flex. Want something intimate — one or two horns walking up to a table or a desk? We can do that. Want the full band showing up to turn an office into a party for ten minutes? We can do that too. Tell us the vibe you're going for and we'll bring the right size group for it.

Booking a Sax-O-Gram

Every quote is built around group size and location, so we don't post a flat rate here — reach out with the details and we'll get you real numbers within a day or two. If you're coordinating a surprise, it's usually easiest for someone other than the guest of honor (a coworker, a partner, a friend) to handle the booking with us.
