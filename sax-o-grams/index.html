Surprise Visits

Sax-O-Grams — A Surprise Worth Remembering.

A Sax-O-Gram is a surprise saxophone visit. Think of it as a live version of a singing telegram, except we show up with actual instruments and real chemistry as a band. Good for offices, parties, celebrations, or anywhere you want to add a moment of live music nobody expected.

How It Works

You tell us who, where, and why.

You tell us who, where, and what you're celebrating. We show up, we play for 5-10 minutes, and we make someone's day. If you're keeping it a surprise, it usually works best if someone other than the guest of honor handles the booking with us so the moment stays genuine.

What to Expect

Quick, fun, and easy.

A small group shows up (usually 2-3 of us), plays for about 5-10 minutes, keeps things high-energy and fun, and then gets out of the way. The whole thing is sized to fit the space you have and the vibe you're going for.

Price is $200, and that covers the group, the travel, and the good time.

Good to Know

Sax-O-Gram FAQ.

When should I book a Sax-O-Gram?

As soon as you know you want to surprise someone. We're pretty flexible on timing, but sooner is always better.

Can you play a specific song?

Absolutely. We've got a deep catalog and we're happy to learn something specific for the right occasion.

Will they actually be surprised?

That's on you to keep the secret. We'll play along and make it special.

What if they don't like saxophone?

Everyone likes a surprise. And everyone likes live music. This hits both.

How do we book?

Fill out our booking form with the date, location, and who we're surprising. We'll follow up within a day or two to lock in details and timing.
